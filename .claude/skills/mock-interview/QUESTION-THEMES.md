# Hiring-Manager Question Themes

These themes are launch points. For each, find the real story in `reference/MEMORY.md` that fits, generate the question live, and let the answer drive the follow-ups. They are anchors, not a script. Never read them verbatim in a row.

**Grounding rule:** every question must be answerable from MEMORY.md. If a theme has no real story to stand on, ask a genuinely open question ("what would you *want* to be doing in this role?") rather than inventing a scenario the user then has to fake.

## 1. Leadership and mentoring
- "Tell me about a time you had to get a team to deliver something none of them had done before."
- "How do you develop someone junior on your team? Give me a specific person."
- Follow-ups: how you handled an underperformer; how you divided the work; what you changed the second time.

## 2. Delivering under deadline or pressure
- "Walk me through the tightest deadline you have owned. What did you cut, and what did you protect?"
- Follow-ups: what was de-scoped; how risk was communicated upward; the moment you decided to build instead of buy.

## 3. Confronting brutal facts / intellectual honesty
- "Tell me about a time the data killed something you had invested in."
- "When have you had to deliver a number nobody wanted to hear?"
- Follow-ups: how stakeholders reacted; whether you were pushed to soften it.

## 4. Cross-functional and customer delivery
- "Tell me about a time you delivered something a customer or partner had to run without you."
- "How do you work with a collaborator whose method you have to productize?"
- Follow-ups: what handing it over cost you; how you handled a failure in the field.

## 5. Decision-making and trade-offs
- "Walk me through a decision where all the options had real downsides."
- Follow-ups: which criteria you weighted; the trade-off you consciously accepted; how you defended the call.

## 6. Ambiguity and initiative
- "Tell me about something you started that nobody asked you to."
- Follow-ups: how you got buy-in with no mandate; what you would do differently; how the knowledge outlived you.

## 7. Failure and learning
- "Tell me about a time something you shipped failed in the field. What did you do?"
- Follow-ups: whether you ran toward the friction or defended the work; what changed in your process.

## 8. Career narrative and motivation
- "Walk me through your career in two minutes." (Assess: a coherent arc, not a list.)
- "Why are you leaving your current role, and why this one?" (Probe for a forward-looking, non-negative answer.)
- Follow-ups: the thread connecting your roles; what you want more and less of.

## 9. Conflict and influencing without authority
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone more senior, or a partner, and had to land it."
- "How do you get people to adopt something when you cannot order them to?"
- Follow-ups: what you did when someone resisted; how you separated being right from being effective.

## Coverage note
A good full session touches leadership, a delivery-under-pressure story, an honesty or failure story, and the career-narrative / "why this role" question. A real hiring manager almost always probes those four. Interleave the rest.
